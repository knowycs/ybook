Innovations

Top Edge Biometrics

User Problem

Traditional fingerprint scanners placed on the keyboard deck require a separate action after opening the laptop, slowing down system login

Solution
  • Seamless UX: Fingerprint scanner positioned on the top edge of the display lid
  • Automatic Authentication: Unlock occurs naturally the moment the user lifts the lid with their finger — by the time the display opens, the OS has fully authenticated the user
  • Smart Battery Protection: Lid angle sensor de-energizes the scanner when closed (0°), eliminating false triggers and battery drain during transport in a bag

Display & Webcam Privacy

User Problem

Ultra-thin lids limit camera optics, built-in lenses add ugly notches or thick bezels, mechanical shutters scratch screens, and loose magnetic cams easily get lost

Solution
  • Uncompromised Optics: Moving camera to dedicated module removes z-height limits for superior sensors
  • Clean Display: Ultra-thin symmetrical bezels on all 4 sides with zero display cutouts
  • Concealed Storage Niche: Push-Push internal chassis cavity for 100% hardware privacy and loss prevention

Space Optimization

User Problem

Built-in speakers and acoustic chambers occupy valuable internal space inside the laptop, while delivering average audio quality due to strict volume limitations

Solution
  • Freed Internal Volume: Eliminating internal speakers reclaims critical chassis space
  • Power Block: +20-30% battery capacity increase
  • Telecom & Cost: Native 5G/eSIM integration and significantly reduced manufacturing weight

Magnetic Prism Module

User Problem

Flat keyboards cause wrist fatigue, while suffocated bottom vents trigger aggressive CPU thermal throttling and deafening fan noise

Solution
  • Ergonomics & Airflow: Elevates chassis for open-channel ventilation and spine relief
  • High-End Audio: Stereo sound with subwoofer, detachable into 2 wireless satellites
  • Power & Status: Integrated bi-directional power bank and optional rear telemetry display
